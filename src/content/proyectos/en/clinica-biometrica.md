## The problem

A telemedicine portal brings scheduling, medical records and remote consultations into one place. This one also lets patients sign in with facial recognition.

## What I built

- Telemedicine API: patients, doctors, appointments and records.
- JWT authentication and a facial-recognition login endpoint.
- Web portal with appointment management and WebRTC video calls.

## How it's built

- Backend in FastAPI and PostgreSQL, documented in Swagger from the first commit and packaged with Docker.
- Frontend in Angular 16 with Angular Material.
